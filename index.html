👻 The Halloween Curse: It is October 31st at WCCC. While exploring the abandoned greenhouse, the heavy oak doors slam shut! Spooky spectral lanterns illuminate the mossy walls. The ghost of 17th-century scientist Dr. Jean Baptiste van Helmont appears, holding a glowing padlock. To escape before midnight, you must apply your knowledge of energy flow, matter cycling, and historic environmental experiments to crack 4 security locks!

🔒 Lock 1: Crypt of the Early Botanists (DOK 1 - Recall & Identification)

Decipher the historical findings carved into the spectral plant pots to find the 3-part key.

Part A: Priestley's Discovery

In 1804, Joseph Priestley placed a mouse and a mint plant in a sealed bell jar. The mint plant "restored" the air. What gas chemical formula was discovered? (e.g., CO2, O2, N2, H2O)

Part B: Light Absorption & Perception

Jan Ingenhousz proved plants require sunlight. Chlorophyll absorbs blue, orange, and red wavelengths. Which color wavelength of visible light do plant leaves reflect, causing us to perceive them as this color?

Part C: De Saussure's Measurement

Nicolas de Saussure grew periwinkles in sealed containers. When plants perform photosynthesis, did oxygen levels INCREASE or DECREASE in the container?

Format Example: [GAS]-[COLOR]-[DIRECTION] (e.g., CO2-RED-DECREASE)

🔒 Lock 2: The Alchemist's Balance & Stoichiometry (DOK 2 - Applied Calculations)

Dr. van Helmont's phantom scale hovers in mid-air. Calculate the precise balances of matter and chemical formulas.

Part A: Van Helmont's 5-Year Willow Experiment

Van Helmont planted a 2.3 kg willow sapling in 90.8 kg of dry soil. After 5 years of watering:

  • Final Tree Mass = 76.8 kg
  • Final Dried Soil Mass = 90.76 kg

Calculations:

  1. How many kg of mass did the tree GAIN?
  2. How many grams of mass did the soil LOSE? (Note: 1 kg = 1000 g)
Part B: Chemical Equation Balance

Examine the balanced chemical equation for photosynthesis:

6CO2 + 6H2O + Sunlight → C6H12O6 + 6O2

How many total molecules of Water (H2O) are consumed to produce 1 molecule of Glucose sugar?

Format Example: [TREE GAIN KG]-[SOIL LOSS G]-[WATER MOLECULES] (e.g., 50.0-100-12)

🔒 Lock 3: The Phantom Energy Pyramid (DOK 3 - Strategic Analysis)

Analyze energy transformations through the haunted ecosystem using ecological laws.

Part A: Productivity Calculation

A ghostly oak forest captures 15,000 kcal/m²/yr of Gross Primary Productivity (GPP) from sunlight. The trees expend 9,000 kcal/m²/yr in cellular respiration (R) to maintain themselves.

Calculate the Net Primary Productivity (NPP) stored as plant biomass using the formula: NPP = GPP - R

Part B: The 10% Trophic Transfer

Using the NPP calculated in Part A as the energy available at the Producer level, apply the 10% Rule to determine how many kcal/m²/yr of energy will be incorporated into the Secondary Consumer trophic level.

Part C: Law of Thermodynamics

Which Law of Thermodynamics (1st or 2nd) states that during every energy transformation, heat is lost to the surroundings and cannot be reused by the ecosystem?

Format Example: [NPP]-[SECONDARY CONSUMER ENERGY]-[LAW NUMBER] (e.g., 10000-100-1)

🔒 Lock 4: Synthesis of Undead Cycles (DOK 4 - System Synthesis)

Synthesize biogeochemical cycling principles to break the greenhouse's master seal.

Part A: Nitrogen Bacteria Diagnostic

Name the specific biological process where specialized soil bacteria convert toxic Ammonium (NH4) into Nitrates (NO3) and Nitrites (NO2) for plant assimilation.

Part B: Phenomenon Identification

Match these 3 environmental phenomena to their process initials (AF, AP, L):

  1. Nitrogen gas (N2) converted to nitrates by lightning or synthetic fertilizer factory. -> [AF] (Abiotic Fixation)
  2. Sulfur dioxide mixing with water vapor to form acid rain. -> [AP] (Acid Precipitation)
  3. Percolating rainwater dissolving soil nitrogen and washing it away into aquifers. -> [L] (Leaching)

Combine the initials in order (1, 2, 3).

Part C: The Anomaly Cycle

Which biogeochemical cycle (Water, Carbon, Nitrogen, Phosphorus, or Sulfur) is unique because it lacks a gas phase in the atmosphere and cycles primarily through weathering of rocks and soil?

Format Example: [PROCESS]-[INITIALS]-[CYCLE] (e.g., AMMONIFICATION-XYZ-NITROGEN)
